the path a report takes

Ground it, seal it, disclose it

01Ground

The claimed version and bug class are checked against curl's advisory record. A claim about a patched or non-existent release is turned away with the reason — before anyone's time is spent.

02Seal

A plausible report opens a private case room with the reproducer, a human security lead and the reporter. Its material is put under embargo, and the chat platform refuses to carry it anywhere else.

03Disclose

The advisory is drafted and held. Only after the human signs off is the embargo lifted and the advisory published — in that order, never the other way round.

Take Switch out and there is nothing left to enforce it. The embargo is not a promise an agent makes. The guard answers Switch's guardrails contract; Switch calls it before every message and refuses any that would carry a case's material into a room the case doesn't belong in. Rooms, roles, the human sign-off — all Switch.
